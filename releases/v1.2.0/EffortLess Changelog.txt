EffortLess - Changelog

v1.2.0
- Added ARMED auto-run on login (default OFF). When armed, EffortLess deposits all & logs out about 6 seconds after every login - the addon half of a hands-off loop (pair it with an external key-sender that presses Enter at character select).
- The 6s login delay is deliberate twice over: it is a Cancel window, and it lets the REAGENTBANK pipe come up (a deposit send racing login is silently dropped).
- On-screen 6s countdown with a "Cancel this login" button; `/el cancel` does the same. Cancelling skips only that login; the addon stays armed.
- New: `/el arm on|off` and an "Auto-run on login (armed)" checkbox in the options panel, with a red on-panel warning.
- Safety: armed is OFF by default; while armed every login logs you out after the countdown. To break the loop from the desktop, delete the EffortLess folder or its SavedVariables.
- Armed auto-run always runs the full action (deposit all, then log out) and skips the manual Yes/No confirm - the countdown is its guard. No change to manual /el, the minimap button, or options. `/reload` suffices.

v1.1.0
- Added an Interface options panel (Interface -> AddOns -> EffortLess) with toggles for the confirm prompt and the minimap button.
- Minimap button right-click opens the options panel (left-click still deposits & logs out); `/el options` also opens it.
- No behavior change to the core deposit/logout action. `/reload` suffices.

v1.0.0
- Initial release.
- One press: deposit all eligible bag items to the Uncapped Vault, then log out.
- Trigger three ways: /effortless, /el, or a draggable minimap button.
- Confirmation dialog before acting (default ON). `/el now` acts immediately; `/el confirm off` turns the prompt off.
- Deposits via the server's VLTDEPALL verb over the REAGENTBANK transport. The server decides eligibility - quest, bound, and kept-rule items stay in your bags by design (not a bug). Works anywhere; no banker or open vault window needed.
- After sending the deposit, waits until bags stop changing (deposits landed) before logging out: 1.2s settle window, 6s hard cap.
- Note: the 20-second logout countdown is enforced server-side. Logout is near-instant only when resting (city/inn) or if the realm has instant logout enabled.
